Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does offline mode work?
FinDiary uses an offline-first architecture. Any transaction or note you log without connectivity is saved locally to your device immediately. Once your internet returns, it automatically syncs in the background with zero data loss.
2. How do I switch my currency?
Go to Settings → Preferred Currency. FinDiary supports USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (₹), JPY (¥), AED, SAR, AUD, CAD, and SGD with automatic formatting.
3. How do Universal AI & Entry Credits work?
Free accounts enjoy 10 diary entries and 5 Arti AI queries daily. When you invite friends via Settings → Invite & Earn, you bank 50 Universal Credits (and your friend gets 25), automatically extending your daily quotas up to 10 extra credits per day.
4. How do I permanently delete my account and records?
Navigate to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account. Confirming will permanently and irreversibly erase your profile, entries, and authentication tokens from our database.
