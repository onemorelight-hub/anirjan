Academic Roots & Purpose

Why Geography Honours at Ramnagar College Shaped My Vision

Geography is not just about maps and landforms. At Ramnagar College, it taught me to observe how people interact with resources, space, and economic friction. When people are displaced from nature and squeezed by extractive systems, their mental peace collapses.

"Anirjan was born from a simple promise: Its about People. Not about corporate vanity. But about cleaning the everyday mess around us so ordinary people can breathe, feel safe, and thrive."

Growing up in Digha gave me an instinctive reverence for freedom, natural beauty, and uncluttered living. That is why every product Anirjan creates carries this quiet, honest simplicity.

With warm intention,
Aparna Dey
Founder & CEO, Anirjan Collective • Jagadishpur, Digha
