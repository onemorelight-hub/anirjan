The Ownership Model

Why We Call It The 1% Club

Most tech companies hoard equity in closed boardrooms while treating community contributors as disposable users. Anirjan was built on an opposite, sacred premise: economic surplus belongs to those who build, distribute, and protect the network.

Under our verified Connect model, 40% of all future platform surplus is legally allocated to reference unit holders. Members of The 1% Club earn reference units by contributing exceptional technological, operational, or strategic value.

💡
Pillar 1: Conviction & Mission Alignment
Zero room for mercenary opportunism. We invite thinkers who genuinely believe in creating a safer, calmer, and freer world.
⚡
Pillar 2: Tangible Craft & High Impact
Whether architecting code, executing growth loops, or advising on governance — 1% seats are earned through demonstrable, measurable excellence.
🤝
Pillar 3: Transparent Pro-Rata Surplus
Reference units entitle contributors to verifiable shares of the 40% surplus pool with zero middlemen fees or predatory dilutions.
