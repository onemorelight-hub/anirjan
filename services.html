Legal Charter • Zero-Tolerance Protocol

Activities We Never Support Under Any Circumstances

At Anirjan, our foundational motto is "Its about People". Technology and local concierge services must safeguard public safety, mutual dignity, and strict lawful compliance. To protect travelers, families, women, local workers, and coastal communities in Digha, we enforce an absolute, non-negotiable prohibition on the following activities:

🚫
Illegal Substances & Contraband
Strict refusal of banned narcotics, psychotropic drugs, party substances, illicit chemical trading, or unlicensed alcohol bootlegging. We maintain zero tolerance across all partner stays.
Immediate Police FIR & Seizure
🚫
Commercial Exploitation & Escorts
Zero tolerance for escort rackets, sex-trade brokerage, child labor, human trafficking, or non-consensual harassment. Any inquiry of this nature triggers immediate logging and criminal reporting.
Strict Criminal Prosecution
🚫
Unauthorized Touts, Dalals & Scalping
Total prohibition on street commission touts (dalals) harassing visitors at Digha station, fake booking vouchers, ticket scalping, deceptive price-jacking, and predatory kickbacks.
Blacklist & Administrative Ban
🚫
Identity Evasion & Forged Papers
We never facilitate guests attempting to bypass statutory tourist verification, register under fake aliases, or present forged ID cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport). Every guest check-in is verified.
Mandatory Govt / Police Verification
🚫
Gambling, Betting & Financial Rackets
Zero support for illegal wagering operations, casino rooms, unauthorized lottery schemes, Ponzi operations, or deceptive financial transactions targeting tourists or locals.
Public Gambling Act Violation
🚫
Coastal Degradation & Beach Harm
Refusal to assist events that dump single-use plastics, create unpermitted high-decibel sound pollution late at night, damage coastal sand dunes, or endanger Bengal's fragile marine ecology.
Environmental Protection Norms
🛡️
Proactive Law Enforcement Coordination
Anirjan operates in full cooperation with the West Bengal Police, Digha Coastal Police Station, and local municipal administrative bodies. If an unauthorized person solicits unethical services claiming affiliation with Anirjan, report it immediately for swift legal action.
