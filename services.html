Activities We Never Support Under Any Circumstances

🚫
Illegal Substances & Contraband
Strict refusal of banned narcotics, psychotropic drugs, party substances, illicit chemical trading, or unlicensed alcohol bootlegging. We maintain zero tolerance across all partner stays.
Immediate Police FIR & Seizure
🚫
Commercial Exploitation & Escorts
Zero tolerance for escort rackets, sex-trade brokerage, child labor, human trafficking, or non-consensual harassment. Any inquiry of this nature triggers immediate logging and criminal reporting.
Strict Criminal Prosecution

Unauthorized Touts, Dalals & Scalping
Total prohibition on street commission touts (dalals) harassing visitors at Digha station, fake booking vouchers, ticket scalping, deceptive price-jacking, and predatory kickbacks.

Identity Evasion & Forged Papers
We never facilitate guests attempting to bypass statutory tourist verification, register under fake aliases, or present forged ID cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport). Every guest check-in is verified.
Mandatory Govt / Police Verification
