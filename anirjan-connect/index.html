Surplus Sharing Architecture

60% Founder — 40% Surplus Distribution

When the Anirjan platform generates distributable surplus or net proceeds, they are shared under a structured 60/40 framework: 60% is retained by the founder for ongoing development and operations, and 40% is shared with eligible contributors and participants according to their pro-rata participation rights.

PLATFORM DISTRIBUTABLE SURPLUS
100% Net Proceeds
FOUNDER / STEWARD
60%
Retained for founder & ongoing platform development
PARTICIPANT POOL
40%
Allocated to eligible contributors & active participants

Worked Example: If Distributable Surplus is ₹1,00,000

Allocation Tier Share % Distributed Amount Recipient Group
Founder Share 60% ₹60,000 Founder / Operating Entity
Participant Distribution Pool 40% ₹40,000 Eligible Contributors & Active Participants
Total Distributable Surplus 100% ₹1,00,000 Total Net Distributable Pool
💡
How Pro-Rata Distribution Works (Ecosystem Pool Allocation) Suppose the eligible participant pool represents 40%. It does not mean every contributor receives 40% of the entire platform's surplus.

Instead, the 40% distribution pool will be shared among eligible participants according to their agreed participation points/units.

For example, if someone holds 1% of the platform reference units, then under a standard pro-rata model they hold 1/40th of the 40% participant pool:
Distributable Surplus: ₹1,00,000
40% Participant Pool: ₹40,000
1% Ecosystem Share: ₹40,000 × (1% ÷ 40%) = ₹1,000 (which is exactly 1% of total surplus)
* Note: Any surplus sharing is strictly based on actual realized net operating surplus and agreed collaboration terms. It is not a guarantee of fixed income or financial return.
