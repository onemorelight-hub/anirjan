Surplus Sharing Architecture

60% Founder — 40% Distributable Pool

When Anirjan generates distributable net surplus, 60% is retained for founder execution and operating entity development, while 40% is shared with eligible contributors according to their pro-rata units.

💡
Pro-Rata Ecosystem Sharing Formula If someone holds 10,000 units (1% of platform total), under the pro-rata pool framework they hold 1/40th of the 40% pool:
Distributable Surplus: ₹1,00,000  |  40% Pool: ₹40,000
1.00% Share = ₹40,000 × (1% ÷ 40%) = ₹1,000 (Exactly 1.00% of Net Platform Surplus)
* Note: Surplus sharing is based on actual realized net operating surplus under a mutual participation agreement.
