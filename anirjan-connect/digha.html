New Digha Sea Beach 🟢 SAFE FOR FAMILIES

Very shallow continental shelf. Safe for walking out and swimming during low tide. Nolias stationed continuously.

Old Digha (Biswa Bangla Ghat) 🟡 EXERCISE CAUTION

Concrete boulders and sea wall. Strong wave undertow during rising high tide. Do not climb slippery wet rocks.

Udaipur Beach (Border) 🟢 OPEN SHORE

Wide open beach extending into Odisha. Water sports, speed boats, and quad biking operating safely today.

Mohona Estuary (Confluence) 🔴 STRICTLY NO BATHING

Dangerous quicksand and unpredictable swirling currents where Champa river meets the sea. Visit only for fish auctions.

🧳 Post-Checkout Freedom • Universal Tourist Guide

11:00 AM Hotel Checkout? Luggage & Return Plan

Almost all Digha hotels mandate morning checkout between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM, but evening return trains depart between 1:00 PM and 6:30 PM. Here is how to safely store your bags, find breezy rest spots, and pre-book a Toto to Digha station.

🚆 Official Railway Facility

Digha Station Cloakroom (PF #1)

Located on Platform No. 1 right next to the Station Master Office. Operates round-the-clock (24/7) with official Indian Railways tariffs.

💰 Tariff: Only ₹20 per bag for the first 24 hours (₹30 subsequent 24h).
🔒 Lock Rule: Bags MUST be securely locked with a padlock or numerical lock. Open zippers/unlocked bags are rejected by railway police.
🆔 ID & Ticket: Must carry valid Govt Photo ID (Aadhaar/Voter) and a confirmed or waitlisted railway ticket.

🌴 Afternoon Rest Spots

Where to Relax Before Your Train

Avoid roaming in the hot coastal midday sun with heavy bags. Recommended breezy rest points:

💺 Station AC Waiting Hall: Free entry on Platform 1 with valid 2S / CC train ticket. Clean seating, ceiling fans, clean restrooms, and phone charging points.
🌊 Biswa Bangla Promenade: Old Digha shaded pavilions right on the sea wall with cool sea breezes and fresh green coconuts (Dab) for ₹35–₹40.
🌲 Amrabati Lake Park: Near New Digha Station Road. Shaded garden pavilions and boating away from beach humidity.

🛡️ Digha Standard Cooking Cap Notice

By local market standard, cooking charges should never exceed ₹50/kg for frying or ₹80–₹90/kg for rich gravy curries. If a hotel cook asks for ₹150–₹200/kg, quote this official benchmark.

🐟 3-Step Freshness & Anti-Formalin Test:
  • Red Gills: Gills must be blood red (pale grey/brown indicates old frozen stock).
  • Clear Eyes: Fish eyes must be clear and bulbous, not sunken or milky.
  • Firm Flesh: Press your thumb on the fish; it should immediately bounce back.

🌅 Tips for Visiting Digha Mohona

Wholesale auctions run between 5:30 AM to 8:30 AM. If you are taking fish back to Kolkata, Howrah, or Kharagpur by train, ask the stall to pack it in a sealed thermocol box with dry ice (costs ₹80–₹120) — your fish remains fresh for up to 14 hours!
