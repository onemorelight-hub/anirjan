Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RupeeDiary.

How does the AI Voice Assistant (Arti) work?

Arti uses advanced natural language processing to listen to your voice and extract financial figures, merchant names, dates, and categories in real time. You can speak naturally like "Spent 1,500 on groceries at DMart" and Arti logs it instantly.

What is Privacy Shield and how does it protect me?

Privacy Shield is our discreet masking engine. When turned on, your account balances, earnings, and sensitive categories (e.g. tobacco, health, lifestyle) are automatically blurred. You can tap any figure to peek at it for 10 seconds using Face ID, Fingerprint, or your PIN.

Can I sync my budget with my actual salary date?

Yes! Under Settings, you can configure your "Salary Cycle Start Day" to any day of the month (e.g., 1st, 5th, 25th) so your monthly budget cockpit perfectly aligns with your pay schedule.

Which languages are supported?

RupeeDiary supports 22 languages natively: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Russian, and Italian.

Can I export my data for tax filing?

Yes. You can download your complete transaction ledger in standard CSV format anytime from Settings > Data & Backup.
